Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who uses this app

Operations Hub is a personal app with one user: its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail: to list and read the owner's own messages inside the app, and to mark messages as read or unread, star or unstar them, or archive them when the owner asks. The app never sends or deletes email.
  • Google Calendar: to show the owner's events and to add or update events the owner creates in the app.
  • Google Drive (read only): to import the owner's own health export files from one chosen folder.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show the owner their own information and to carry out actions the owner asks for.
  • Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server run by a third party, other than Google itself.
  • Data is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • No person other than the owner reads the data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Operations Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting Google in the app's settings also revokes the access at Google and deletes the stored access tokens.
